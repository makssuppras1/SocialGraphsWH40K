9. KEY RESEARCH FINDINGS
================================================================================

FINDING 1: Network Structure Encodes Semantic Themes, Not Faction Labels
- Network communities align better with semantic clusters than with portals
- Communities span many portals (mean 5.8) but fewer semantic clusters
- Strong community-cluster alignments (e.g., Community 3 → Cluster 13)
- Conclusion: Hyperlinks reflect narrative/semantic relationships, not 
  organizational/faction structure

FINDING 2: Semantic Coherence Explains Faction-Community Misalignment
- High semantic coherence factions (Inquisition 83.6%, Adepta Sororitas 78.7%) → Better 
  community alignment
- Low semantic coherence factions (Space Marines 12.5%) → Poor community 
  alignment
- Mechanism: When factions are semantically diverse, network communities 
  split them following semantic themes rather than faction labels

FINDING 3: Mythology vs Narrative Distinction Partially Supported
- Weak negative correlation (-0.0667) between betweenness and consistency
- Major figures (Guilliman, Emperor) have high betweenness but moderate 
  consistency (0.49-0.57)
- Some narrative agents (Oan Mkoll) have high betweenness with low consistency
- Conclusion: The distinction exists but is not as clear-cut as hypothesized

FINDING 4: Primarchs as Mythological Anchors
- Primarchs dominate top betweenness rankings
- They appear in Cluster 2 (Primarchs cluster)
- Moderate consistency suggests they bridge diverse parts of lore
- They act as structural and semantic bridges

FINDING 5: Narrative Agents Form Coherent Semantic Clusters
- Gaunt's Ghosts characters cluster in Clusters 13/14
- Space Wolves cluster in Cluster 7 (highest consistency: 0.6055)
- These narrative-focused groups have high semantic coherence
- Network communities align well with these clusters

10. ANSWER TO RESEARCH QUESTION
================================================================================

"To what extent does the hyperlink-based character network encode the 
faction structure described in textual lore?"

ANSWER: Partially, but with important exceptions.

- The network encodes faction structure ONLY to the extent that factions 
  have semantic coherence
- High semantic coherence factions (Inquisition 83.6%, Adepta Sororitas 78.7%) → 
  Good alignment with network communities
- Low semantic coherence factions (Space Marines 12.5%, Chaos 16.1%, Imperium 16.0%) → 
  Poor alignment with network communities
- Overall: Network encodes semantic/narrative structure more than 
  faction structure

"What network mechanisms explain alignment or misalignment?"

ANSWER: Semantic coherence is the key mechanism.

1. When factions have high semantic coherence (Portal ≈ Semantic Cluster):
   - Characters share similar text themes
   - Network communities align with faction because links follow semantic 
     themes
   - Example: Inquisition (83.6% alignment), Adepta Sororitas (78.7% alignment)

2. When factions have low semantic coherence (Portal ≠ Semantic Cluster):
   - Characters span multiple semantic themes
   - Network communities split the faction because links follow semantics, 
     not labels
   - Example: Space Marines (12.5% alignment, spans 16 clusters)

3. The Mechanism:
   - Hyperlinks reflect narrative relationships (who appears together in 
     stories)
   - Narrative relationships follow semantic themes (similar characters 
     appear together)
   - Faction labels are organizational categories (may not match narrative 
     themes)
   - Therefore: Network structure encodes semantic/narrative structure, 
     not necessarily faction structure
